A Client-First Approach to Justice

Jordan Anhorn is a dedicated personal injury attorney who understands that a severe accident turns a client's life upside down. Bringing years of high-stakes litigation experience from her tenure at esteemed firms like Genco Injury Attorneys and Matlin Law, Jordan founded Anhorn Law to provide highly personalized, aggressive representation for those injured by the negligence of others.

Dual-licensed to practice in both Colorado and Florida, Jordan handles a wide array of complex personal injury matters, focusing heavily on catastrophic injuries, severe auto collisions, and commercial trucking accidents.

Leadership in Trucking Litigation

Commercial vehicle litigation requires a highly specific skill set. Recognizing her authority in this complex area of law, Jordan serves as the Co-Chair of the Colorado Trial Lawyers Association (CTLA) Trucking Safety Committee. In this role, she helps educate other attorneys on the nuances of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) regulations, electronic logging device discovery, and corporate liability structures.

She is also an active and proud member of the Women Trial Lawyers Network, a testament to her commitment to professional excellence, continued legal education, and advocacy inside and outside the courtroom.

Why Choose Anhorn Law?

Jordan believes that effective legal representation requires absolute transparency and direct communication. When you hire Anhorn Law, you are not handed off to an inexperienced associate or paralegal—you work directly with Jordan to build a strategic roadmap for your case.

She knows exactly how insurance companies evaluate claims, and she prepares every case as if it is going to trial. This meticulous preparation is what forces fair settlements and delivers justice for her clients.
